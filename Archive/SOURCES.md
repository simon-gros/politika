## Political-history source collection (69 indexed developments)

The [political-history narrative](POLITICAL_HISTORY_1980_1989.md) and [machine-readable event register](POLITICAL_EVENTS.csv) distinguish verified historical dates, contested accounts, sources' institutional perspectives and optional game-design interpretations. They add to the earlier 31 art/reference records without turning political speech, rights reports or museum materials into shippable assets.

**Federal leadership:** [Slovenska biografija's item on Janez Drnovšek](https://www.slovenska-biografija.si/oseba/sbi1024150/) documents the 15 May 1989 appointment and rotating Presidency of SFRY, a constitutional event especially sensitive to POLITIKA's living-Tito counterfactual.

**Federal and economic:** [IMF history of Yugoslav stabilization](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9781557759719/ch013.xml), [contemporary IMF review of the 1981–83 arrangement](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9780939934492/back-1.xml), [Yugoslav inflation 1989](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/1991/050/article-A001-en.xml), and [UPI's 16 May 1982 contemporary report on Milka Planinc's election](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1982/05/16/First-woman-prime-minister-elected-in-Yugoslavia/5387390369600/). These establish high-level governance and economic events, not exact factory shapes or invented black-market prices.

**Serbia, Kosovo and Vojvodina:** [Human Rights Watch's historical Kosovo report](https://www.hrw.org/reports/2001/10/26/under-orders-war-crimes-kosovo), [earlier HRW chronology](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/1992/yugoslavia/), [peer-reviewed Kosovo miners research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/nationalities-papers/article/ethnopolitics-in-a-mining-enterprise-in-crisis-revisiting-the-albanian-miners-protests-in-late-socialist-kosovo/1F3B02E90DEF0490C8C2E2B11202822B), [RFE/RL's Reuters political timeline](https://www.rferl.org/a/1066641.html). [This movement-affiliated historical archive](https://uckarchive.org/en/history/1981-1989) is a supplementary interpretive source, **not** an independent arbitration of legality, victim numbers or security-service culpability. Compare texts from multiple communities, the contemporaneous press and constitutional historians.

**Slovenia:** [Government of Slovenia historical account](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/history-of-slovenia/), [constitutional history chronology](https://nastajanje-ustave.si/preface.0.html), [1987 Litostroj workers' strike from the state archives](https://www.gov.si/en/news/2019-06-01-france-tomsic-and-the-litostroj-workers-strike/), [8 May 1989 May Declaration](https://www.gov.si/en/news/2020-05-08-the-may-declaration-is-a-symbol-of-the-slovenian-peoples-desire-for-sovereignty/), [historical museum entry for DEMOS](https://enciklopedija-osamosvojitve.si/clanek/predsedstvo-demosa/). These separate civil-society movements, the republic's government, the federal army and the broader public.

**Croatia:** [Croatian encyclopaedia's political-party chronology](https://www.enciklopedija.hr/clanak/hrvatska-drzava), [Croatian state-history overview](https://croatia.eu/index.php/en/home-en/history/contemporary-croatia) and [HDZ's own party-history record](https://www.hdz.hr/category/82/povijest-stranke). The party's account is primary testimony about its own founding, not an independent description of political legitimacy.

**Bosnia and Herzegovina:** [Parliamentary Assembly's 1945–1990 history](https://www.parlament.ba/Content/Read/182?lang=en&title=ParlamentarizamuBosniiHercegoviniuperiodu1945.%E2%80%931990.), [1983 Sarajevo Trial account from the Izetbegović Foundation](https://www.alijaizetbegovic.org/en/anniversary-of-the-beginning-of-the-1983-sarajevo-trial/) and [the 1987 Agrokomerc affair chronology with journalist testimony](https://www.media.ba/bs/magazin/afera-agrokomerc-30-godina-poslije-svjedocenje). Some material is directly connected to historical participants; distinguish recorded proceedings from their retrospective interpretation.

**Macedonia:** [Peer-reviewed comparison of Macedonian and Slovenian elite transformation](https://doi.org/10.4000/balkanologie.281) documents the late-1989 reformist turn and allows a sourced Macedonian entry instead of generic federation-wide assumptions. Further locate actual 1989 Skopje party congress records and primary newspapers before specifying exact speeches or propaganda designs.

**Montenegro:** historical changes in Titograd are covered via [the HRW Yugoslav historical report](https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/1992/yugoslavia/) and Serbia–Montenegro late-1980s political research, but Montenegro-specific assembly minutes and period photography remain a particularly useful next acquisition.

**External political and diplomatic:** [Non-Aligned summit documents](https://www.nam.go.ug/previous-chairmanship-documents), [UN 1989 Belgrade summit records](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/80583), [CSCE/OSCE Madrid–Vienna conference timelines](https://www.osce.org/mc/43198), [1989 CSCE Vienna final document](https://www.osce.org/mc/40881), [European Communities 1980s history](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/history-eu/1980-89_en), [NATO INF Treaty](https://www.nato.int/en/what-we-do/wider-activities/nato-and-the-inf-treaty), [German federal archive history of the Berlin Wall opening](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/themen-entdecken/online-entdecken/storys/die-stasi-im-jahr-1989/november-1989/), [German account of the Hungarian–Austrian border opening](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/schwerpunkte/deutsche-einheit/ungarn-oeffnet-seine-grenze-337804), [Bulgarian News Agency's Zhivkov chronology](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/archives/1005141-november-10-1989-todor-zhivkov-is-ousted-as-bulgarian-communist-party-chief), [Human Rights Watch on Bulgaria's Turkish minority](https://www.hrw.org/reports/1989/WR89/Bulgaria.htm), [Albanian leadership history](https://enciklopedija.hr/clanak/hoxha-enver) and [Romanian December 1989 history](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/december-22/romanian-government-falls).

## Yugoslav historical map research — 172 indexed map references

The [cartographic guide](MAPS_1980_1989.md), [map catalogue](MAP_CATALOG.csv) and [map-only index](media/reference-only/maps/INDEX.md) document three contemporary original maps from 1981, one 1981-revised aeronautical compilation, earlier Yugoslav road atlases, later/undated topographic series, and 44 individual geographical sheet leads.

**Authentic period items:** [1981 CIA political Yugoslavia map](https://www.loc.gov/item/2011587053/), [1981 CIA shaded-relief Yugoslavia map](https://www.loc.gov/item/2011587054/), and [1981 Zagreb State Department city map in UT PCL](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/world_cities.html).

**Earlier baseline:** [Military Geographical Institute 1962 road atlas, 36 sheets](https://kartevgi.hismus.hr/en/karte/road-map-of-the-socialist-federal-republic-of-yugoslavia/) and [1974 road sheet with Slovenian/Croatian city insets](https://kartevgi.hismus.hr/en/karte/road-map-sheet-of-the-socialist-federal-republic-of-yugoslavia/).

**Topography:** [Former Yugoslavia DMA Series M709 GeoPDF collection](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/topo/former_yugoslavia/) and [coverage grid](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/topo/former_yugoslavia/index_map.jpg). Some sheets were published in 1995–1996; no map's publication date may be inferred from the series label. [UT Austin's country index](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/yugoslav.html) includes other pre- and post-period mapping.

**Still-to-inspect period publications:** [1985 Yugoslav yearbook](https://publikacije.stat.gov.rs/G1985/Pdf/G19852003.pdf) and [1989 yearbook](https://publikacije.stat.gov.rs/G1989/Pdf/G19892003.pdf) may contain useful cartographic graphics, but no individual map pages have been authenticated in this research pass.

## High-value primary artefacts and original imagery

**[Visual Archive of Yugoslavia — Museum of Yugoslavia](https://vaju.muzej-jugoslavije.org/)** (first exact-period object indexed as `POL-1980-BEL-TERAZIJE-001`). Search by `1980`, `1984`, `1988`, object signature, person, city and photographer. It offers actual collection records, photographers, dates and rights fields; the photographic record for Milan Simić's early-1980 Terazije series is especially promising for transport, everyday city life and fuel rationing. Request authorization before reproduction.

**[Wikimedia Commons: 1982 Sarajevo tram photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:1982_in_tram_transport_in_Sarajevo)** — four dated photographs by Milan Suvajac are already individually catalogued as `POL-1982-SAR-TRAM-001` to `004`. Commons states CC BY-SA 4.0 on each file page; credit, licence, modifications and share-alike duties follow from the exact file licence. Crucial for overhead wires, Tatra K2 tram silhouettes, 1982 streets, tram maintenance, roadway surface and vegetation. This is the best current exact-period image cluster.

**[dLib.si — Digital Library of Slovenia](https://www.dlib.si/)** — period newspapers, magazines, ephemera and issue-level metadata with persistent URNs. Catalogued: [Mladina, 5 April 1984](https://dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ADOC-E01O55YN), [Katedra, 9 Nov 1985](https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3Adoc-35EY0YN9), [Mladina 1986, issue 25/26](https://dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ADOC-PR77030L), [Glasbena mladina 1988/89](https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ADOC-NSFGFEC5). Some Mladina scans require designated reading-room terminals. Metadata is not proof that page images have been inspected. Search in Slovene by `Katedra`, `Mladina`, `TAM`, `Iskra`, `samoupravljanje`, `plakati`, `stanovanjska naselja`, `obnova`, `inflacija`, `stavka` and each town name.

**[Museum of Yugoslavia — Relay of Youth, 1987](https://muzej-jugoslavije.org/en/art/stafeta-mladosti/)** and **[House of European History — 1987 poster affair](https://historia.europa.eu/en/our-work/news/behind-poster-day-youth)** — object-level and explanatory sources for socialist ritual, media controversy, experimental graphic design and late-period ideological symbolism. *Important*: the notorious Novi Kolektivizem poster was selected but not actually plastered on streets; don't model a city covered in it.

**[Olympic Museum item 23850](https://olympic-museum-artefacts.zetcom.net/en/collection/item/103918/)** — 1984 Vučko poster: specific object, artist, materials, colours, size and museum reproduction limits. **[Museum in Exile's Sarajevo '84 design collection](https://www.museuminexile.com/index.php/olympics84)** documents graphic standards, accreditation, signage, pictograms and ticket design. **[Olympic Committee of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://okbih.ba/en/text/wog-84/75)** gives the event and urban construction context. **[Olympics.com venue overview](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/sarajevo-1984-olympic-venues-overview)** is useful for venue geography but many present-day photographs are NOT authentic 1984 condition.

**[United States Olympic Committee 1984 report, LA84 Digital Library](https://digital.la84.org/digital/collection/p17103coll6/id/6531/rec/2)** — contemporary outsider perspective on security at the Sarajevo Olympic Village; subject to the report's viewpoint and reporting accuracy, and not a basis for historical claims about the game's fictional conspiracy.

**[UN Digital Library — Belgrade Non-Aligned summit final documents](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/80583)** — primary conference documents from 4–7 September 1989, transmitted in UN document A/44/551 / S/20870. The [Non-Aligned Movement's summit archive](https://www.nam.go.ug/previous-chairmanship-documents) provides the 1983 New Delhi, 1986 Harare and 1989 Belgrade continuity. Official records prove an institution's public stance; they are not neutral third-party verification of any historical political claim.

## Historical research and contextual authorities

**[Slavic Review — unemployment and crisis, 1965–1984 (1989)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/slavic-review/article/abs/systemic-changes-and-unemployment-growth-in-yugoslavia-19651984/ED652204DE3D6B46A7F10D943DB8CD00)** and **[IMF — Yugoslav Inflation and Money (1991)](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2016/12/30/yugoslav-inflation-and-money-927)**. Useful for plausible import shortages, currency instability, industrial constraints, black-market pressures and civic anxiety, *not* evidence for exact costs of fictional units. Dates in publication and historic-period analysis differ.

**[Government of Slovenia historical overview](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/history-of-slovenia/)** gives an institutional account of the 1988 JBTZ trial and civil demonstrations. **[Nationalities Papers — Trepča miners 1988–1989](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/nationalities-papers/article/ethnopolitics-in-a-mining-enterprise-in-crisis-revisiting-the-albanian-miners-protests-in-late-socialist-kosovo/1F3B02E90DEF0490C8C2E2B11202822B)** is a peer-reviewed account of protest and enterprise life. **[Oral History Kosovo: 1989 miners' strike](https://oralhistorykosovo.org/research/miners-strike-1989/)** gives first-person testimony collected retrospectively. Keep differing political perspectives visible and attribute assertions rather than treating any one institution's narrative as neutral ground truth.

**[New East Digital Archive — Yugoslav new wave (1981 and aftermath)](https://www.new-east-archive.org/features/show/12495/yugoslav-new-wave-1980s-music-40-years-on)** — cultural-history interviews and references to `Paket aranžman`, alternative music, clothing and print design. For production, create *original* period-plausible art and sound; do not import protected recordings or album art.

**[1985 tourism study — Adriatic coast and foreign earnings](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0261517785900202)** offers a genuine contemporary analysis of Yugoslav tourism, not imagery for any particular hotel. **[Antibureaucratic revolution academic survey](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/nationalities-papers/article/abs/thirty-years-of-yugoslavias-antibureaucratic-revolution-a-longrun-appraisal-and-new-avenues-of-research/1A228C18012D46DB2589A84999398D29)** provides contextual interpretation of 1988–1989 Serbia, Vojvodina and Montenegro mobilizations; date-specific photography is still needed.

**[Archives of Yugoslavia photographic holdings](https://www.arhivyu.rs/en/arhivska-gradja/fotografije)** and **[Yugoslav Film Archive](https://en.kinoteka.org.rs/archive/)** — *collection-level leads*, not verified 1980s items. Archives of Yugoslavia's overview explicitly names many pre-WWII collections; obtain item date, place, signature and rights before catalogue promotion.

## Underrepresented regions: targeted acquisition plan

The initial catalogue is intentionally uneven; it must **not** be mistaken for adequate all-Yugoslavia coverage.

- **SR Croatia / Adriatic**: seek 1980–1989 Zagreb, Rijeka, Split, Dubrovnik and tourist-industrial coast *item-level* references. Begin with [National and University Library Zagreb periodicals](https://nsk.hr/fond/novine-i-casopisi/) and library catalogues; digitised `Stare hrvatske novine` largely covers *much earlier* periods, so do not assume 1980s scans are freely available. Search `Vjesnik 1985`, `Slobodna Dalmacija 1984`, `Jugoplastika`, `Rade Končar`, `Brodogradilište`, `hotel`, `Jadranska magistrala`.
- **SR Serbia / SAP Vojvodina**: prioritize Belgrade's period traffic, Novi Sad, factories, agricultural co-operatives, Danube bridges, urban signage, buses and 1988–1989 demonstrations. Look first to the Museum of Yugoslavia's photographic records and local municipal museums.
- **SR Bosnia and Herzegovina**: extend strong Sarajevo 1982–1984 evidence with Tuzla industry, Zenica steelworks, Mostar, hydroelectric sites and railway yards. Keep Olympic and non-Olympic Sarajevo distinct.
- **SR Macedonia (then name)**: [State Archives' Skopje branch](https://arhiv.mk/en/arhivska-mreza/) lists over 30,000 city-area photographs *dating only through 1983*, plus enterprise records through 1990. Excellent 1980–1983 lead only, **not** proof of 1984–1989 coverage. Prioritize Skopje centre, transport, industry and housing.
- **SR Montenegro**: Titograd (period name), Bar, Kotor, coastal tourism and ports, rail links, 1988–1989 politics. Preserve `Titograd` for 1980s display, with `Podgorica` as modern search synonym.
- **SAP Kosovo**: Priština/Prishtina, Trepča/Trepça, rail/mining systems, labour, residential environment and 1988–1989 civic politics; represent Albanian and Serbian perspectives and use multilingual place search terms.
- **SFRY international links**: Italy, Austria, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece and Albania border installations, ferry ports, cross-border vehicles and trade; do not claim all neighbours were military combatants in the actual 1980s.

## Search, verification and use protocol

1. Search each institution *and* item, record stable URL/URN/signature, creator, historic event date, creation date and access constraints.
2. Separate `contemporary`, `near-period-secondary`, `retrospective` and `collection-lead`. A 2025 city photograph is not a 1985 street texture reference even if the building survived.
3. Where a page contains multiple photos, cite the **specific item**; avoid assigning the collection's headline date to all images.
4. Mark each observation as direct visual evidence, textual factual report, interpretation or fictional extrapolation. Verify with a second independent source for high-impact claims.
5. Add a stable catalogue ID and candidate use (`tram-units`, `UI`, `urbanism`, `mission`), rights status and confidence.
6. Map the use to [Story Outline](../Docs/STORY_OUTLINE.md), [Concepts Outline](../Docs/CONCEPTS_OUTLINE.md) or a specific gameplay system without asserting that the game has implemented that content.
7. Do not copy third-party source content into the Git repository without explicit compatible redistribution rights and attribution.

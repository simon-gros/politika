# POLITIKA — Archive media

This folder deliberately holds **metadata first, binaries later**. The first online research pass catalogues dated period material and source leads without mirroring protected photographs, newspapers, posters, film or audio.

- [Reference-only index](reference-only/INDEX.md) is the browseable, sourced collection. Items may be visible online yet not licensed for redistribution or game inclusion.
- `licensed/` is reserved for future redistribution-cleared reference files and any accompanying item-specific manifest. There are no binaries committed as part of this seed.
- The [rights and provenance guide](../RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md) determines what may be imported or released.

Follow Marburgum's source-first workflow: discover, verify, classify, rights-check, model an original adaptation, record derivation, and review in engine. Always keep historical evidence separate from alternate-history canon.

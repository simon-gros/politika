## Evidence and interpretation cautions

- Primary period artefacts and contemporary press establish the perspective and date of their record; a source does not automatically validate every contemporary claim.
- **A** means a dated primary item or contemporary documentary record; **B** means credible retrospective scholarly or institutional reporting; **C** means constrained, interested or incomplete interpretation; **D** identifies collection leads, not verified 1980s items.
- A date span can represent a **process**, a **reporting interval**, a **collection research target** or an **approximate event month**. Inspect `date_precision` and notes before assigning a specific day to a game event.
- Kosovo autonomy, casualty figures and the legitimacy of some constitutional proceedings remain disputed. Sources from different organizations and communities must be read comparatively, not merged into a single unquestionable viewpoint.
- Official republic narratives, party histories and human-rights organizations each have distinct institutional perspectives. The narrative of a source is not automatically game canon.
- Historical Tito died in 1980. POLITIKA's fictional Tito survived until assassination in 1984, followed by a fabricated public continuity and 1989 revelation; these are not historical claims.
- The federation's actual multi-party elections and wars mostly occurred in 1990–1992; they must never be represented as historical events of 1989 merely because the *fictional* campaign ends in war in 1989.
- **No redistributed source-media binaries were added.** All linked historical photographs, documents and content require item-specific licence review before reproduction.

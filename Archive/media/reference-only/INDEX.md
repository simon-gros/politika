## Historical interpretation boundaries

Yugoslavia's actual 1980–1989 history differs from the game's fictional timeline. Tito died in 1980 in actual history, while POLITIKA deliberately fictionalizes survival to 1984 and later assassination and concealment. Modern vector/cartographic reconstructions do not imply 1980s original publication. The 1988-looking map released in 2008 is explicitly a modern map.

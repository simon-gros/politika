## Acquisition and permissions

- Obtain period 1980–1989 editions first; check printing dates, independent creator/publisher, scale and sheet identifier.
- Library catalogue records and yearbook citations are **not scans**. For official topographic series, series production dates are **not proof of individual sheet import**.
- Do not assume Commonwealth, EU, Yugoslav government, LOC or Wikimedia access automatically clears redistribution. Verify image-level rights; the original map and a new digital scan can have different conditions.
- M709 sheet names and modern place names are pointers to physical geography. Compare period records before depicting streets, police jurisdictions, factories or political borders in POLITIKA.

# POLITIKA — Industry, Civilian Infrastructure and Everyday Life, 1980–1989

**Research pass: 29 October 2026.** This guide links authenticated period photographs, period ephemera and contemporary publications to *original* RTS environmental design. It complements the [Master Catalogue](CATALOG.csv), [Map Catalogue](MAP_CATALOG.csv), [Regional Cartographic Atlas](REGIONAL_CARTOGRAPHIC_ATLAS.md) and [Rights and Provenance](RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md). The sources are historical evidence; references to possible RTS systems are suggestions, not claims that assets have been produced.

## 1. Strongest original industrial-photo collection: Prvi maj, Pirot

The Historical Archive in Pirot has supplied named 1983 and 1985 photographs via Wikimedia Commons, with verified VRTS correspondence (`2018102410001786`) and **CC BY-SA 3.0** on the five individually indexed files. These are stronger production references than modern architectural reconstructions because the images are described as actually taken during the game decade. **Dates and rights still belong to each file**, and the original photographer is not individually credited in the supplied records.

| Source ID | Dated original | Subject | Design evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `POL-1983-PIROT-PRVIMAJ-YARD` | [1983 factory courtyard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dvoriste_Prvog_maja.jpg) | Industrial yard and circulation spaces | Yard form, pedestrian/vehicle access, factory wall and paving |
| `POL-1983-PIROT-PRVIMAJ-PARKING` | [1983 parking lot](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parking_Prvog_maja.jpg) | Parking outside factory | Period civilian car silhouettes, car placement, industrial frontage |
| `POL-1985-PIROT-PRVIMAJ-COMPLEX` | [1985 factory complex](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kompleks_fabrike_Prvi_maj_Pirot.jpg) | Overall complex buildings | Building massing, connecting spaces, worksite facades |
| `POL-1985-PIROT-PRVIMAJ-RESTAURANT` | [1985 company restaurant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Restoran_Prvog_maja.jpg) | Employee dining amenity | Interior scale, furnishings, social-space props |
| `POL-1985-PIROT-PRVIMAJ-SPORT` | [1985 company sports ground](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sportski_teren_Prvog_maja.jpg) | Company recreation facilities | Outdoor leisure, fences and social amenities |

**Period takeaway:** Yugoslav industry encompassed social, recreational and commercial services alongside production. Representing a factory only as a combat target or resource icon would erase part of the historical environment. Potential neutral simulation features: workers entering/leaving, civilian road congestion, workforce canteens, company parking, and noncombatant facilities. None need become targets or objective points merely because they can be represented in a game map.

**Rights takeaway:** CC BY-SA 3.0 permits reuse with its obligations; an open-licence photo does not automatically grant rights to the enterprise's name, logo or architectural design, or make a derivative game compatible with share-alike without review. The archive keeps source links only in this research pass and has not added any photo binaries to the UE project.

## 2. A genuine period vehicle reference: 1983 TAM truck

[Chris Jantsch's 1983 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tovarna_Automobilov_in_Motorjev_(TAM)_110_T10.jpg), `POL-1983-TAM-TRUCK-110`, documents a **TAM 110 T10** built in Slovenia and photographed in Yugoslavia in 1983. The creator labels the image **CC BY-SA 4.0**. It supplies valuable silhouette, proportions, chassis/cabin details and period truck materials for vehicle concept art and non-combat traffic.

The photograph does not confirm the precise scene location and a license-built vehicle model may have protected badges/trademarks. A simplified fictional truck should be original art with separately designed branding, and gameplay movement/collision values should come from engine metrics rather than a photographic guess.

## 3. Belgrade civilian economy and municipal representation

[1985 STR Hercegovina grocery-business card](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STR_Hercegovina_,_Momir_A._Kova%C4%8Devi%C4%87_,_Belgrade.jpg), `POL-1985-BELGRADE-STORE-CARD`, is described as period ephemera with a **modern 2019 digital photograph**, licensed CC BY 4.0 by Jovan M. Kovačević. Distinguish its claimed 1985 paper date from the photograph metadata. It is useful for vernacular commercial documents, storefront typography and local economy settings.

[Stevan Kragujević's portrait of Branko Pešić](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stevan_Kragujevic,_Branko_Pesic,_15,_januar_1985.JPG), `POL-1985-BELGRADE-KRAGUJEVIC-PESIC`, is dated **15 January 1985** with **CC BY-SA 3.0 Serbia** and credited family permission. Useful for archival government-office photographic culture and period public notices. Do not infer Pešić was actively serving as mayor at the date of this portrait.

Two distinct [1985 concert photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dire_Straits_1985_Mark_Knopfler_Alan_Clark_Jack_Sonni.jpg) of Belgrade's Hala Pionir, including [John Illsley](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Illsley_1985.jpg), illustrate arena lighting and civic leisure. Their Commons source pages identify **Zoran Veselinović** and **CC BY-SA 2.0**. Both depict the *same 10 May 1985 concert*, not separate historical events. They provide mood/architecture, not permissions to use musicians' likenesses in game assets.

## 4. Sarajevo 1984 civic construction and 1989 popular culture

The [Sarajevo Organising Committee's official 1984 report](https://digital.bgs.ba/zavrsni-izvjestaj-ok-xiv-zimskih-oi/), `POL-1984-SARAJEVO-OLYMPICS-OFFICIAL-REPORT`, is a 198-page multilingual (Serbo-Croatian, English, French) period-original publication. The [LA84 Foundation edition](https://digital.la84.org/digital/collection/p17103coll8/id/11863/) is published online with IOC permission; individual source-map plates still require inspection. The local Sarajevo digital library simultaneously states that its digitized contents are for personal/research use and restricts downloading, copying and commercial use without permission despite listing the bibliographic item as public domain. **Respect the restrictive display and reuse terms for that particular hosting service.**

This official source provides verified context for urban venues, transport planning, accommodation, civic messaging, and the interface/wayfinding aesthetic of Sarajevo at the game's alternate-history opening. The [IOC's modern venues overview](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/sarajevo-1984-olympic-venues-overview) is a later illustration *about* those 1984 facilities, not an original period printed map.

The Commons [Zetra 1989 rock-marathon photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%C5%BD.A._%C4%8Ci%C4%8Dak,_M.V._Tifa,_B._Langer,_D._Gobac_i_B._%C4%90uri%C4%87,_%C4%90uro,_YU_rock_maratona,_Zetra_1989.jpg) is **captioned as 1989 but its uploaded file was created in 2016**. The photograph's original shutter date and ability to license the underlying historical image were not independently established. It is therefore a lower-confidence research lead, and the catalogue deliberately identifies this discrepancy.

## 5. Demography and settlements: 1981 census, 1991 publication

The federal [1981 census municipality and settlement tables](https://publikacije.stat.gov.rs/G1981/Pdf/G19814001.pdf), `POL-1981-CENSUS-1991-PUBLICATION`, use the **31 March 1981** territorial distribution and include municipality/locality tables. The introduction to the available volume is dated **19 June 1991**. Therefore the dataset's *census date is 1981*, while *the referenced publication is later*; it should not be presented as an original 1981 map or volume.

For POLITIKA, settlement population and urban/rural classifications can inform the distribution of neutral civilian populations, urban density and municipal services. Ethnic/linguistic data are **not** proxies for individual political conviction, trustworthiness, military faction membership, threat, or violence. The game’s fictional warring factions should not be mapped automatically onto historical ethnicity. Any hypothetical geocoded population layer must state its spatial resolution, publication date, source limitations and modern processing assumptions.

The [Commons 1981 census map collection](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:1981_census_of_Yugoslavia) contains modern derivative graphics rather than a verified bundle of maps printed in 1981. Their cartographic interpretations and licences vary.

## 6. Follow-up source trails and explicit gaps

[Belgrade by year](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Belgrade_by_year), [Sarajevo by year](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Sarajevo_by_year), and [Yugoslavia in the 1980s](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Yugoslavia_in_the_1980s) provide discoverable sets with potentially relevant period photographs but require individual file/date/rights verification. More coverage is needed for original 1980s **Ljubljana and Maribor factory and street photographs, Zagreb commercial buildings, Skopje municipal industry, Titograd/Bar port facilities, Vojvodina farm cooperatives, Kosovo/Trepča mining infrastructure**, and everyday transport in rural settlements. Prioritize local institutional archives, rather than multiplying undated map tiles.

### Proposed asset provenance handoff (design only)

1. Record source ID, *image-created date* vs online upload date, item provenance, original photographer, exact licence and the image features being studied.
2. Produce an original neutral environment concept, vehicle silhouette, road-junction sketch or UI image. Keep independently designed emblems, public-service notices and noncombatant visuals as separate layers.
3. For an imported derivative image, establish permission/attribution/share-alike compatibility; document any alteration and in-game distribution conditions.
4. Author RTS terrain and interaction data separately from visual art: enterable/impassable buildings, road network, bridges, civilian areas, neutral industry, no-combat zones and faction ownership remain explicit design choices.
5. Validate the resulting map's time-specific civilian and administrative appearance against the [historical cartographic atlas](MAPS_1980_1989.md). Do not infer fictional POLITIKA outcomes from archival photographs.

**Repository status:** these entries are source-linked metadata only, not direct 3D models, original in-game photographs, confirmed map collision meshes or rights-cleared shippable assets.

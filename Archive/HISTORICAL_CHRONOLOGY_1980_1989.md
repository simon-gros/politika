## Alternate-history transition rules

- **Clock A — historical evidence**: a real dated image can inform a fictional city block in 1984 only when its physical form plausibly persists from that actual date and no later modification is falsely projected backward.
- **Clock B — alternate POLITIKA setting**: Tito survives beyond 1980, is assassinated in 1984, a small Unaligned inner circle hides his death using distinct body and voice doubles, and the conspiracy is exposed in 1989. Only these are canon; never insert them in historical evidence fields.
- **Clock C — mission/gameplay timeline**: faction campaigns and skirmish missions can vary, and speculative mission hooks in the archive are *proposals*, not implemented systems or final narrative canon.
- Historical economic crisis, autonomy disputes, external diplomatic relations and ordinary culture can inspire the world without mechanically reproducing the actual sequence of events.
- The Unaligned is the game's *fictional AI-administered neutral regime*, not a claim that every historical Yugoslav military or civilian institution participated in cover-ups, manufactured intelligence or knowingly manipulated faction wars.
- Where real persons or governments appear, keep fictional wrongdoing attached to fictional characters. Do not label historical student magazines, Kosovo miners, soldiers or regional protesters agents of the fictional Ascendancy/Pact/Concord/Directorate.
- The Sarajevo Olympics, Tito public appearances, Day of Youth ritual, 1988 trials and 1989 Belgrade diplomatic summit are **divergence-sensitive events** and require campaign-level confirmation before appearing unchanged in scripted history.

## Priority narrative compatibility checks

1. Assign any public Tito appearance a **fictional timeline provenance**: authentic pre-1984 game-Tito, post-assassination *body double*, or archival footage from before the fictional murder; audio can instead involve the separate voice double. Establish evidence chains and falsification metadata.
2. Keep *independent sources* in missions: newspaper issue, radio edit master, military dispatch, hospital record or witness interview. Multiple references should be needed to prove the false public leader, as in the Story Outline.
3. Historically credible administrations have different jurisdictions and interests: municipal workers, republican leaders, federal military, local police, unions and ordinary civilians cannot all share a single secret and objective.
4. Distinguish **historical journalism and propaganda** from **Unaligned-distributed fake in-game intelligence**. A planted report is a game artifact and must not appear as historical fact or as a real 1980s newspaper scan.
5. Tie environment changes to year: road resurfacing, staged public ceremonies, growing protest signs, shortages or price updates are possibilities; document each *actual* occurrence separately before claiming period accuracy.

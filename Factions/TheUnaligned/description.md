# The Unaligned

## Overview

The fifth and final ideological faction of POLITIKA is the Unaligned, an exceptional, exclusively non-playable faction representing the political administration, governmental institutions, security apparatus and armed forces of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia. Unlike the four principal factions, which function as independent playable geopolitical and military powers, the Unaligned serves as the principal administrative authority within the game's fictionalized version of 1980s Yugoslavia. Its existence reflects the distinctive political and historical position of Yugoslavia during the Cold War: the development of an independent socialist system under Josip Broz Tito, the country's separation from Soviet political domination and its extensive relationships with both Eastern and Western powers. Within POLITIKA, the Unaligned provides the institutional, political and military framework through which the activities of the four competing factions interact with the territory, population, economy and sovereignty of Yugoslavia.

## Ideological Foundations

The ideological foundations of the Unaligned are rooted in Titoism, Yugoslav socialism, workers' self-management, socialist federalism and the principles of international nonalignment. Although Yugoslavia emerged from the communist revolutionary movement of the Second World War and maintained a political system dominated by the League of Communists of Yugoslavia, its subsequent development differed considerably from the highly centralized Soviet model.

Following the Tito–Stalin split of 1948, Yugoslavia pursued an independent political trajectory characterized by:

- The gradual decentralization of economic administration
- The introduction of workers' councils
- The development of a distinct socialist economic system
- The establishment of diplomatic and economic relationships extending across ideological boundaries

These historical characteristics form the foundation of the Unaligned's political identity, distinguishing its institutions and administrative behavior from those of the Red Directorate, despite their common socialist origins.

## Political Structure

The Unaligned's governmental structure reflects the constitutional organization of the Yugoslav federation, incorporating the six constituent socialist republics of Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro and Macedonia, together with the autonomous provinces of Vojvodina and Kosovo within Serbia. Federal institutions coexist with republican, provincial and municipal administrative structures, producing a complex system of political responsibilities, regional jurisdictions and institutional coordination.

The federal government exercises authority over important questions of national defense, foreign relations, monetary affairs and broader constitutional administration, while regional institutions retain substantial responsibilities concerning economic development, public services, infrastructure and local government. These arrangements are represented through an interconnected network of governmental buildings, administrative headquarters, municipal institutions and territorial authorities distributed throughout the game world.

### The Legacy of Tito

The political legacy of Josip Broz Tito occupies a central position in the faction's historical and ideological background. His leadership established the principal institutions of Yugoslav socialism, promoted the doctrine of Brotherhood and Unity among the federation's constituent peoples and positioned Yugoslavia as a prominent participant in the international Non-Aligned Movement.

In real history, Tito died on 4 May 1980. POLITIKA diverges from that chronology: in its fictional timeline, he survives the illness that killed him historically and continues to appear as Yugoslavia's leader during the 1980s. The nature of the federal leadership's subsequent continuity, the authority exercised in Tito's name, and the hidden events of 1984–1989 form the central mystery of the campaign. For the complete spoiler-filled explanation, see [the story outline](../../Docs/STORY_OUTLINE.md).

### Resource Jurisdiction and Meridian Starting Locations

The game's **highest-valued resource group is nuclear materials**, followed by **fossil fuels**. Unaligned authorities administer territory, infrastructure, electricity networks, and sites that may include crude-oil and natural-gas deposits. Meridian Pact players **always start near a fossil-fuel source field**, but this does not transfer control of the field from the Unaligned or other legitimate owners. Access, policing, commerce, and military security still follow the world's neutral-AI rules.

## Economy

The Unaligned administers a distinctive system of socialist self-management, combining socially owned enterprises, workers' councils, decentralized industrial decision-making, regulated market mechanisms and federal economic coordination. Its industrial and commercial infrastructure includes manufacturing enterprises, agricultural cooperatives, transportation companies, energy facilities, mining operations, financial institutions and regional trading organizations.

Unlike the economic systems of the principal playable factions, the Unaligned's industries primarily function as components of Yugoslavia's existing domestic economy rather than as instruments of a player-directed military production system. Factories, warehouses, railway terminals, electrical substations, telecommunications facilities and commercial centers form part of the administrative and economic environment with which other factions must interact. Their operations may influence resource availability, territorial development, infrastructure access and the broader economic consequences of military or political intervention.

## Military and Defence

The Unaligned's military organization is represented principally through the Yugoslav People's Army (JNA), together with the Territorial Defence system and relevant internal security institutions. Its military doctrine reflects the historical Yugoslav concept of Total People's Defence, which emphasized the combination of conventional armed forces, territorial mobilization, decentralized resistance and the broader participation of society in national defense.

The JNA maintains conventional armored formations, mechanized infantry, artillery, military aviation, naval forces, engineering units and strategic installations throughout the federation. Territorial Defence organizations provide additional capabilities through locally organized formations, reserve mobilization, defensive preparations and geographically dispersed military resources. These institutions allow the Unaligned to protect strategically significant territory, respond to security emergencies and resist attempts to undermine Yugoslav sovereignty.

## Internal Security and Civil Administration

Internal security and civilian administration constitute equally important dimensions of the faction's activities. Police forces, border authorities, customs administrations, security agencies and municipal institutions maintain a visible presence throughout the game world. These organizations regulate access to restricted facilities, administer border crossings, enforce governmental regulations, investigate unauthorized activities and respond to disturbances affecting public order.

Government buildings, military installations, checkpoints, transport hubs and industrial facilities may be protected by personnel operating under different levels of administrative authority. The interaction between civilian and military institutions creates a functioning political environment in which foreign factions cannot simply operate throughout Yugoslav territory without encountering legal, administrative or security consequences.

## Gameplay

In practical gameplay terms, the Unaligned is controlled entirely by the game's artificial intelligence and underlying simulation systems. It is not available through conventional faction selection, does not possess a standard player-directed campaign and does not pursue territorial conquest according to the same strategic objectives as the four playable powers. Instead, its institutions administer the game world's existing political and territorial organization, responding dynamically to the activities of competing factions. The Unaligned controls important governmental centers, military bases, civilian infrastructure, public services and administrative jurisdictions, creating a persistent institutional presence whose behavior influences the strategic opportunities available to players.

### Sovereignty and Graduated Response

A central gameplay mechanic involves the relationship between foreign military activity and Yugoslav territorial sovereignty. The Unaligned may tolerate certain diplomatic, commercial or military arrangements while opposing unauthorized troop movements, attacks upon civilian infrastructure, attempts to occupy governmental installations or operations that threaten federal security. Its response depends upon the character and severity of the activity, the political circumstances of the affected territory and the wider strategic situation:

- **Minor incidents** may produce administrative warnings, inspections, restrictions or diplomatic consequences.
- **Serious violations** may result in police intervention, military mobilization, reinforcement of defensive positions or direct engagement by Yugoslav armed forces.

This graduated system of authority makes the Unaligned a dynamic participant in the game world without requiring it to behave as a conventional expansionist opponent.

### Diplomacy and Cooperation

Diplomatic and economic interactions with the Unaligned provide important strategic opportunities. Players may seek authorization to use transportation infrastructure, establish commercial agreements, obtain access to industrial resources, negotiate logistical arrangements or develop political relationships with Yugoslav institutions. The success of such activities depends upon existing diplomatic relations, economic considerations, regional political circumstances and the player's previous conduct.

Cooperation with administrative authorities may improve access to important facilities or reduce restrictions upon certain operations, while hostile activities may lead to increased surveillance, the suspension of agreements, asset restrictions or the deployment of additional security forces. These interactions provide alternatives to direct military confrontation and encourage players to consider the political consequences of their decisions.

## Internal Contradictions

The Unaligned's institutional behavior is also shaped by the internal contradictions of the Yugoslav federation. Economic difficulties, regional disparities, administrative decentralization, competing republican interests and the tensions associated with collective political leadership may influence the effectiveness of governmental responses. Different regions possess distinct economic priorities, political relationships and levels of institutional stability, although all remain part of the broader Yugoslav administrative framework.

Such differences create opportunities for dynamic events involving industrial disputes, infrastructure disruptions, political negotiations, public demonstrations and conflicts between regional and federal authorities. These developments contribute to a more complex simulation of Yugoslav society while avoiding the reduction of its political history to a simple confrontation between foreign military powers.

## International Position

The Unaligned's international position reflects the practical meaning of Yugoslav nonalignment rather than an absolute absence of foreign relationships. Its institutions maintain diplomatic, economic and technological connections with multiple ideological blocs, including states and organizations possessing sharply different political systems. Within the alternate-history setting of POLITIKA, this permits the Unaligned to cooperate selectively with different playable factions while preserving its independent administrative authority. Such cooperation does not automatically imply ideological allegiance, military subordination or permanent geopolitical alignment. The essential characteristic of the faction is its attempt to maintain sovereign decision-making within a political environment increasingly influenced by competing foreign interests.

## Non-Nuclear Authority and Processing Infrastructure

The **Unaligned is non-nuclear and NPC-only**. It has no playable Intimidation ability, while the Ascendancy alone may coerce eligible isolated hostile units; non-hostile Yugoslav troops and civilians are not freely convertible. The Ascendancy must **fully develop its limited espionage capabilities** before infiltrating Concord/Directorate nuclear research facilities. The Atlantic Concord and Red Directorate are the two established nuclear powers: both can refine crude nuclear-material game resources and use the processed resource for **nuclear power plants and weapons**. The **Iron Ascendancy starts non-nuclear**, must steal nuclear-refining and weapons intelligence from Concord/Directorate research facilities, may refine only for **its first bomb**, and **cannot construct nuclear power plants**. Completing that first bomb wins the active game immediately, without deployment.

The **Meridian Pact cannot refine nuclear materials at all** but can steal and potentially deploy an existing completed weapon. Its starting position always lies near an existing oil or natural-gas field, without automatically controlling it. Crude oil must be refined into petroleum fuels or kerosene in an eligible refinery; nuclear power plants owned by eligible factions can provide electricity to game networks. The Unaligned responds to unauthorized activity and defends territorial jurisdiction through normal neutral AI rules, without becoming a player nuclear faction.

## AI Propaganda, Public Continuity and Population Morale

The **Unaligned has its own distinct advanced propaganda apparatus**, operated exclusively by **NPC/system AI**, not a human player. **Radio, newspapers and television** are its primary media. Federal broadcasts, official reporting and staged public appearances sustain continuity and public order in its fictional setting. A restricted security inner circle uses the apparatus to conceal Tito's assassination, relying on the separate voice and body doubles, while most media staff and institutions remain unaware of the actual conspiracy.

**The Unaligned also has a population morale meter** maintained by the world simulation. As with every faction, higher morale increases **production output and army discipline**, influencing the simulated Yugoslav economy and its armed forces. Neutral media buildings and audiences retain jurisdiction and sovereignty rules: propaganda infrastructure is not freely commandeered merely because a player approaches it. See [Propaganda and Morale](../../Docs/PROPAGANDA_AND_MORALE.md).

## NPC Formation Support and Discipline

**Terminology:** NPC military personnel use **Discipline**, while civilian/non-combat personnel use **Morale**, with a **single shared resolve-meter model** underneath. Combat formation support affects Discipline, not civilian Morale. Unaligned still has faction-wide **Population Morale**. An Ascendancy intimidator can convert only an **eligible isolated unit whose Discipline (combat) or Morale (non-combat) has first been fully broken**; civilian or neutral status does not automatically grant eligibility. See [Espionage and Intimidation](../../Docs/ESPIONAGE_AND_INTIMIDATION.md).


Unaligned military personnel gain ordinary battlefield Discipline stability in **larger, mutually supporting formations**; individual troops and tiny detachments suffer the usual Discipline vulnerability. Unlike the **Red Directorate and Iron Ascendancy**, the Unaligned does **not** gain a separate bonus merely for operating a very massive coherent army. **The Atlantic Concord alone** avoids the small-group penalty. The Unaligned also retains an NPC-governed population morale meter. Iron Ascendancy Intimidation may convert **eligible isolated combat units after their Discipline is fully broken**, or **eligible isolated civilian/non-combat units after their Morale is fully broken**. Neutral or non-hostile Unaligned personnel are **not automatically eligible**; the simulation must enforce authored sovereignty and target-eligibility rules. These checks belong to the neutral world AI and authoritative simulation. See [Propaganda and Morale](../../Docs/PROPAGANDA_AND_MORALE.md).

## Strategic Identity

From a game-design perspective, the Unaligned occupies a fundamentally different position from the Atlantic Concord, Red Directorate, Iron Ascendancy and Meridian Pact. While those four factions possess independent strategic objectives, player-controlled military structures and competing approaches to warfare, the Unaligned functions as the persistent administrative and territorial foundation of the game world. Its institutions establish the legal, political, economic and security conditions under which the principal factions operate. Players may encounter it as a cooperative administrative authority, a neutral territorial presence, a diplomatic intermediary or an armed opponent responding to violations of Yugoslav sovereignty, depending upon their actions and the circumstances of the campaign.

The Unaligned is ultimately conceived as a simulation of Yugoslavia's federal administrative system, political institutions and national defense structures rather than as a fifth conventional military superpower. Its purpose within POLITIKA is to provide the game world with a functioning indigenous government whose institutions possess their own responsibilities, limitations and capacity to respond to external developments. By representing Yugoslavia as an independent political actor rather than merely as disputed territory, the Unaligned establishes the distinctive historical and geopolitical foundation of POLITIKA: a fictionalized Cold War environment in which four competing ideological powers operate within and around a sovereign socialist federation attempting to preserve its political independence, institutional continuity and territorial integrity.

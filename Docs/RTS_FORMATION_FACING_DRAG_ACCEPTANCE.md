# FORM-FD — Right-drag formation facing and frontage: acceptance record

**Date:** 1 November 2026 (implemented 31 October) · **Scope:** the facing/width control left open by [FORM-F3](RTS_FORMATION_F3_LIFECYCLE_ACCEPTANCE.md#e-not-covered) and §9 and §12 of [Military Formations, Tactical Stances, Cohesion and Discipline](RTS_UNIT_FORMATIONS_AND_DISCIPLINE.md): one right-mouse drag sets a group's destination, facing and frontage (files per rank), for ordinary group moves (client-planned slots, one admitted Move per unit) and for a selected persistent formation (one formation Move planned by the server). Previous baseline: `49598e9` (FORM-F6 part two, seven shapes, `network_protocol` 8). Tested on the multiplayer skirmish test map at its cap of 20 units per participant.

**Outcome: IMPLEMENTED and ACCEPTED for the skirmish test map. Packaged facing-drag check 53/53 in two runs (click, Line/Column/Perimeter/Echelon Left drags, Escape and LMB cancel, Shift+RMB, single unit and a formation drag, all driven by real input; byte-identical normalized plans); packaged shape check 46/46 in two runs; Automation 47/47; regression matrix unchanged from the FORM-F6B baseline; the protocol-8 client is now refused.**

## A. What changed (feature `c1e2169`, harness `0e646f6`, Automation cases `e694e34`)

- **Gesture.** With **two or more** commandable own units selected, an RMB press on ground no longer orders at once: it becomes pending. Release below the selection system's DPI-aware drag threshold (`politika::selection::ExceedsDragThreshold`, the same as the LMB box) is the old click: same point, travel facing, default frontage, one batch of ordinary Moves. Past the threshold it is a **facing drag**: the HUD shows a preview and the release sends one order. A hostile target, a non-ground hit, a single selected unit and **Shift+RMB** (reserved for a future queue) still act on press, and their release sends nothing. Every press yields exactly one click, one drag commit, or nothing.
- **Geometry** (`Source/PolitikaCore/FormationGeometry.h`, `DragToFacing`/`ShapeDrag`, map-relative XY in cm, no Unreal types). For ground-hit start S and end E: D = E − S, L = |D|, **anchor A = (S + E)/2**, **facing F = (D̂y, −D̂x)**, so the existing `RightOf(F) = (−Fy, Fx)` equals D̂: the drag runs along the front rank from its left end to its right end. The skirmish camera looks along +Y with screen-right at world −X, so a left-to-right screen drag faces the block away from the camera (+Y); the reversed drag gives the same anchor and frontage and the opposite facing. **files = clamp(round(L/spacing) + 1, 1, members)**, rounding half away from zero (`std::lround` in C++, `f64::round` in the Rust harness), with L/spacing bounded before conversion. A drag shorter than one spacing keeps the facing and anchor but uses the default frontage (0). Zero length, NaN/∞ points and non-positive or non-finite spacing give no facing; the gesture then falls back to the click if the press point is valid, otherwise it cancels. The four-argument signature in the brief cannot clamp to the member count, so the helper takes the member count as a fifth input.
- **Frontage by shape.** Line, Column and Dispersed use the override as their files per rank; Echelon Left/Right build the Line block at that width and then turn it 45 degrees (one rotation, no second width transform); Wedge and Perimeter keep their own geometry and only take the facing (the sender sends frontage 0 and the core ignores any value for them). Zero keeps the F2/F6 defaults (Line 10, Column 2, Dispersed 5, Echelons as Line). Slot assignment, spares, the two-legality-checks-per-member budget and input-order independence are unchanged.
- **Formations** (`Source/PolitikaCore/Formation.h`). `Registry::PlanMove` takes an optional fixed facing and frontage and stores them with the anchor (`explicitFacing`, `frontage`). A regroup after a loss re-plans with the stored facing and the stored frontage clamped to the survivors. A later plain Move (no drag) resets both, so the block faces its travel direction at the default width again. Release, dissolve and a new Form start without the old geometry.
- **Network.** `FPolitikaNetFormationIntent` gains `FacingX`, `FacingY` (float, like the destination) and `Frontage` (uint8, ≤ `kMaxFormationMembers` = 64). Move and AttackMove accept facing exactly (0, 0) (travel facing) or a finite unit vector within 1e-3; frontage must be 0 without a facing. Form, Release, Stop, SetStance and SetAdherence require all three zero. `CheckIntentShape` rejects anything else as malformed before any state changes, so a rejected request leaves the anchor, facing, frontage, member orders, routes, stance and adherence untouched. **`network_protocol` 8 → 9**; a protocol-8 client is refused at join. Ordinary group moves send no new fields: they remain per-unit Move intents with client-planned slots.
- **Server.** A dragged formation Move is validated, planned and fanned out by the existing server path (`PolitikaFormations.cpp`): one server plan with the requested facing and frontage, then one ordinarily admitted Move per member. The client preview is advisory only.
- **Preview and HUD.** The held drag reuses `GetGroupMoveMarkers`/`GroupMoveMarkers` (no second marker system) and the same `PlanLocalGroupMove` as the commit, so a group preview and its committed plan are identical for the same inputs. Re-planning happens at most every 0.1 s and only after the cursor moved more than 4 px; each re-plan stays within two legality checks per member (40 for 20 units). Release commits from the **last sample taken while the button was held**, never from a cursor read in the release handler. Cancel clears the preview at once (it never lingers for the 2.5 s order-ring time). The HUD line next to the drag reads, for example, `PREVIEW · LINE · 5 files · drag to set facing / width · Esc cancels`, or `PREVIEW · Formation 1 · LINE · 4 files · …`; a Wedge or Perimeter shows `facing only` and `drag to set facing`. Command outcomes after the release are the existing ones.
- **Cancellation.** Escape (which then does not open the menu), an LMB press, viewport focus loss, the match leaving Active, a generation change, losing the controller binding or participant, an emptied or changed selection, a changed whole-formation identity, an unusable endpoint, and a release that never reaches the binding (button up for more than 0.25 s, as for the LMB stroke) all cancel with no intent sent.
- **Diagnostics** (non-Shipping). `RTS_GROUP_MOVE_DRAG` (start/update/commit/cancel with screen and world endpoints, anchor, facing to 1e-6, frontage, replans, checks, reason); `RTS_GROUP_MOVE_PLAN` gains `drag`, `explicit_facing`, `requested_frontage`, `frontage`, `slots`; `RTS_FORMATION_SENT`, `RTS_FORMATION_INTENT` and `RTS_FORMATION_MOVE` log facing, frontage and the stored values.
- **Tests.** Portable suite: drag direction, reversal, vertical/diagonal drags, unit facing, short/threshold/zero drags, NaN/∞, bad spacing, 1 and 64 members, half-spacing rounding, clamping, determinism; frontage overrides for Line, Column, Dispersed and both Echelons (span, centred partial rank, separation, budget, order independence, the Line before the turn and the held-back flank), Wedge/Perimeter unaffected; registry store, regroup reuse and clamp, plain-Move reset, release/re-Form, foreign participant; intent-shape acceptance and rejection. Automation `Politika.RTS.Multiplayer.GroupMoveFacing` (cases 1–22 of the brief, plus a held drag cancelled by viewport focus loss and by the match leaving Active, run through the real controller, match and server formation path). Packaged: `rts_multiplayer --group-move-facing`.

## D. Decisions taken for §9 and §12

- **Drag semantics:** the drag line is the front rank, traced from its left end to its right end; the anchor is its midpoint (not an end, the camera, or the group's centroid). Facing is perpendicular to it by the existing `RightOf` convention, so no world-space sign is inverted to suit the camera.
- **Two thresholds:** the screen threshold separates click from drag; the world threshold (one spacing) separates "facing only" from "facing and width".
- **Width only with a facing:** a non-zero frontage without a facing is malformed, so width cannot be injected on its own.
- **Regroup:** keeps the last dragged facing and width; any plain Move restores travel facing and default width.
- **Save/net schema:** formations are not saved; `network_protocol` 9.

## E. Not covered

- RMB-drag Attack-move (the network schema carries it; Q keeps its gesture), Shift+RMB queueing, waypoint queues, rotation keys or mouse-wheel rotation, per-soldier arrival facing and any facing-based combat effect.
- A packaged casualty and regroup after a drag (Automation and the portable suite cover it), a packaged Dispersed, Wedge or Echelon Right drag (Automation), and packaged focus-loss/phase-change cancellation (Automation `GroupMoveFacing` covers both; the packaged run covers Escape and LMB).
- Reinforce, speed matching, Layered Screen, Convoy, gamepad, touch and other platforms.
